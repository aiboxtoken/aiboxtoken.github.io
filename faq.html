FAQ

Before you start

Clear expectations make a stronger launch.

Do you need my seed phrase or private key?

No. Never send a seed phrase or private key. Critical transactions should be approved from a client-controlled wallet.

Are liquidity funds included?

No. Liquidity capital is supplied separately by the project owner. Network and third-party fees are also separate unless stated in the project scope.

Can you guarantee listing, price or profit?

No. We provide technical launch services only and do not guarantee exchange listings, token price, trading volume or investment returns.

Can the website run on GitHub Pages?

Yes. Static launch sites work well on GitHub Pages and can later use a custom domain.

Can you integrate a buy/swap interface?

Yes, when technically appropriate. Wallet/mobile compatibility depends on the selected integration and available liquidity.

What does the client receive at handover?

Depending on package: token addresses, transaction references, website source, transparency links and final launch documentation.

Can existing projects hire you?

Yes. We can scope upgrades such as website, transparency, liquidity, vesting or wallet UX separately.

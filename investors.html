POSSIBLE STRUCTURES

How participation may be structured

Structures below are discussion concepts only. No fixed return, repayment or token entitlement is being promised.

  • Equity partnership: a negotiated ownership interest, with dividends only from lawfully distributable profits if approved.
  • Project revenue participation: a capped, contract-based share of actual eligible project cash receipts, potentially with no payout during underperformance.
  • Strategic JV or acquisition: negotiated commercial terms, asset or share transaction, milestones and governance rights.

Potential USDC settlement

If permitted after legal, tax and banking review, qualified counterparties may discuss settlement using native USDC on Solana. Any transaction requires signed documents, verification of funding source, corporate treasury approval and sanctions/KYC checks. No wallet is provided for deposits here.

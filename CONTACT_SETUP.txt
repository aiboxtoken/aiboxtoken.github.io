AIBOX DIRECT CONTACT FORM — ONE-TIME SETUP

1. Create a new Google Sheet.
2. Open Extensions > Apps Script.
3. Paste the complete contents of google-apps-script.gs.
4. In Apps Script, open Project Settings > Script Properties.
5. Add TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN and TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID.
6. Select Deploy > New deployment > Web app.
7. Execute as: Me.
8. Who has access: Anyone.
9. Copy the Web App URL.
10. Open contact-config.js and replace:
    PASTE_GOOGLE_APPS_SCRIPT_WEB_APP_URL_HERE
    with the Web App URL.
11. Upload contact.html and contact-config.js to the website.

Customers can then submit the form without opening LINE.
The green LINE button remains available for customers who prefer direct chat.
